# Formiva Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Status | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-017 | A working synthetic demo will make later founder-led outreach more effective than concept-only outreach. | Unverified | Demonstration feedback and later discovery interviews |
| A-018 | Customer discovery is deferred, not cancelled. | Accepted by ADR-17 | 15–20 interviews and discovery-gate review |
| A-019 | The demo can be built and tested without real personal data. | Required constraint | Synthetic corpus validator, secret scan, and review evidence |
| A-020 | The current infrastructure and provider decisions remain unchanged while the demo is scaffolded. | Accepted unless separately changed | ADR and decision-log review before deployment |
